# ARE 504 past exams: search results

Checked **17 October 2026**. Requested period: **2023–2024**, roughly two to three years before the current midterm.

**No verified matching exams were found.** The public sources checked did not yield an authentic, accessible KFUPM ARE 504 midterm paper from 2023 or 2024. No exam PDFs were downloaded, and no past-exam questions are included in the study guide. This finding does not establish that older papers do not exist.

## Course match

The [official KFUPM graduate bulletin](https://bulletin.kfupm.edu.sa/course-details?level=Graduate&subject_code=ARE) identifies **ARE 504: Deep Renovation for HP Buildings**. An [official AECM program document](https://aecm.kfupm.edu.sa/docs/default-source/default-document-library/are-gp-seie-report-programs-courses.pdf?sfvrsn=ea40c43e_1) lists the same code under the earlier title **Deep Renovation of Buildings**. Both sources were publicly readable; neither is an examination paper.

The search used both title forms, `ARE 504` and `ARE504`, KFUPM, the instructor's name, midterm/exam terms, years and semester codes. It covered public university and faculty pages, search-indexed student resources and broader web results. Papers from other universities or different KFUPM courses were excluded, even when a course number matched.

## What would qualify for the separate collection

A future paper should have a verifiable **KFUPM** header, **ARE 504** code, deep-renovation subject, **midterm/major exam** designation and a date or semester corresponding to **2023–2024**. Its source and legitimate access status should also be recorded. The course LMS or an instructor-approved paper from a previous student would be a useful next source.

Any accepted papers should stay separate until requested. Revision priorities in the guide are inferred from the supplied lectures and exercises; they are not predictions based on past exams.
